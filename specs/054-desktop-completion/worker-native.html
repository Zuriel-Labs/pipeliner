Acceptance criteria

A dirty source, missing/private-path mismatch or incompatible host refuses before native provisioning. No host mount, forwarded key/agent or renderer access is added. The original typed-input injection and temporary-input cleanup checks pass through the actual runtime. Five distinct bounded commands reach readiness concurrently; their values and process identities remain scoped. Every native result is tied to the exact clean source/tree. Success requires verified removal of owned containers, guest workspaces, VM, helper processes and fixture. A failure records the exact stage and keeps uncertain resources.
