Acceptance criteria

  • Fixed destinations, no environment/CLI token fallback, no bundled secret/private key, no secret-bearing log, and abort/expiry prevent dispatch.
  • Provider poll interval, slow-down and refresh are followed; tokens remain trusted and device/auth failures are sanitized. All outstanding credential flows terminate on cancellation.
  • Wrong scope, stale epoch/policy/head, unknown tool, Ready changes/replacement and incomplete state produce no mutation. Active statuses are exactly In Progress, In Review and Pending Review.
  • Every operation group has actual authorized personal/organization results or a visible blocker; no broad-token or branch-control bypass. No required product capability is silently reduced.
  • Local configured suite and exact-head PR checks pass; resource ownership/cleanup is verified. App selection and live fixture consent are not inferred from repository engineering authentication.
