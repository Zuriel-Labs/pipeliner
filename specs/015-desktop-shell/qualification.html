This bounds shell feasibility without implementing Desktop product workflows. Final Draft 1.0 remains the product baseline; original draft files and framework adopters are preserved.

Retain Electron provisionally; do not mark it qualified yet. Its packaged helper, IPC restrictions, resize/dialog/recovery and synthetic response measurements passed on this Mac. Investigated Tauri's official process/sidecar documentation after the minimize failure. No Tauri app was built. Because the same minimize request also failed in plain AppKit, current evidence does not establish an Electron-specific defect or show that switching frameworks fixes it. The root cause remains unknown. Resolve native behavior before the framework decision is accepted; a material direction change requires PM direction and a documented baseline revision.
