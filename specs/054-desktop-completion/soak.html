Acceptance

A soak pass requires at least 86,400,000 monotonic milliseconds, complete initial/final samples, no observation gap exceeding two sampling intervals (120,000 ms), all original workload/native assertions, unchanged captured authority and zero additional model/catalog/write effects. Cached navigation p95 remains below 2,000 ms; sampled local navigation control feedback remains below 150 ms. Publish cold/warm traces and resource measurements without inventing a memory target. Any interruption, failed assertion or missing evidence is incomplete/failure, never a shortened pass.
